Frequently asked questions

The fifteen things people ask most about ClearDeck: why it uses a VPN, whether it needs root, what it collects, how many rules ship inside it, and how “traffic saved” is actually calculated.

  • Android 10 – 17
  • v1.0.1
  • 428,423 rules

  1. Is this a VPN? Why does it need a VPN connection?

    ClearDeck uses Android’s built-in VPN interface for one reason: it is the only way an ordinary, non-root app can see every other app’s connections. The VPN interface is a local tunnel — traffic is inspected on your phone and then goes straight out. There is no remote node and nothing is relayed.

  2. Does my traffic go through your servers?

    No. Nothing leaves the device. Connections are judged against the rule lists on your phone: a match is rejected immediately, and everything else goes straight out over your normal connection. There is no remote node and no server that relays or stores your traffic.

  3. Do I need root?

    No. ClearDeck runs as a normal app and asks only for Android’s standard VPN authorization. No root, no modified system files, no unlocked bootloader — so OTA updates are unaffected. Some banking or security apps may simply note that a VPN is active; that is their own detection policy, not a root check.

  4. Does it work on my phone?

    It supports Android 10 – 17 on 64-bit ARM devices.

  5. Are there ads in the app?

    No. The app carries no advertising and needs no account.

  6. What exactly gets collected?

    Usage statistics plus crash and performance reports, and only after you accept the privacy notice on first launch. The content of your traffic is never collected. The full list is in the privacy policy.

  7. Why does the app ask me to accept a privacy notice before it will run?

    Consent is a hard gate: the notice appears on first launch, and if you decline it confirms once and then the app will not run. The gate ensures that any component which might process data starts only after you agree. Either way, your traffic never leaves the device.

  8. How many rules are included, and does it need to download them?

    Five built-in modules totalling 428,423 entries, about 9.5 MB inside the app. They ship with it, so a fresh install works offline. Afterwards the app checks for newer lists on its own and downloads only the modules whose version actually moved. You can also import your own file or rule link.

  9. What is “traffic saved” and is it accurate?

    It is an estimate, not a measurement. Each blocked connection is priced at what the same domain delivered per allowed connection; a target that was only ever blocked falls back to a fixed 16 KiB. That means it can over- or under-state the real figure — treat it as a rough idea of what you avoided, not an exact count.

  10. Will it slow down my phone or drain the battery?

    Measured on a OnePlus 6T: 30 minutes of protection costs about 1.148% of one core. A power saving mode lowers the refresh rate and pauses deep inspection, at the cost of recording fewer short-lived connections.

  11. What is deep inspection and do I need it?

    Deep inspection lets ClearDeck see the full address before deciding, instead of just the domain, and it needs a certificate installed on the phone. Many people do not need it — domain rules alone block most things — and it does nothing until you turn it on and install the certificate. Only the first 256 KB of each connection is checked.

  12. Can I block or allow a single app?

    Yes. In an app’s access log every record has Block and Allow on the right; tapping either opens an “Apply to…” chooser where you can scope the rule to that app only or to all apps. What you add collects in My block & allow on the modules screen.

  13. Where do the rule lists come from?

    Ad rules come from EasyList, EasyList Ad Servers, 217heidai/adblockfilters and AWAvenue Ads Rule; tracker rules from EasyPrivacy, AdGuard Tracking Protection and AWAvenue Ads Rule; malware rules from Hagezi Threat Intelligence Feeds (TIF, mini). The lists ship with the app for offline use, and the sources are listed inside the app.

  14. Can I export my data?

    Yes — the tools screen exports the access log as CSV. Records are cleared when protection stops, so export before you stop it if you want to keep them. There is no cloud sync and no ClearDeck account.
