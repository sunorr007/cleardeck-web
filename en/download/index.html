Get ClearDeck

ClearDeck 1.0.0 is released and rolling out to app stores. This page covers where to find it, how the four builds differ, and what to know before installing.

  • v1.0.0
  • 446,709 rules
  • Android 10 – 17
status

Released · v1.0.0

Rolling out to app stores. Search for “ClearDeck” (御流 in the Chinese UI).

How to find it

  1. Search your app store for “ClearDeck”, or 御流 in the Chinese UI.
  2. Check that the developer is Beijing Fenghong Technology Co., Ltd. (北京烽鸿科技有限责任公司).
  3. If it does not appear yet, that store’s rollout is still in progress — check again in a few days.

Before you install

Price
The core app is free and carries no in-app advertising; ad blocking, deep ad blocking and malicious site blocking are membership modules
Membership
Huawei AppGallery payment in the Huawei build, Alipay in the OPPO build and the Xiaomi app store (app joint operation) in the Xiaomi build; the Google build includes no purchase
Trial
The first time you enable any restricted module you get a 14-day trial
Account
No ClearDeck account is needed
Devices
Android 10 – 17, arm64-v8a only
Rules
446,709 bundled, usable offline from a fresh install; tracker modules are on by default and restricted modules are off

The four store builds

One app, four builds, one per app store. The features are identical; they differ only in the analytics SDK and in whether membership can be purchased.

Google

no analytics SDK and no membership purchase

OPPO

no analytics SDK; membership is purchased through Alipay

Huawei

includes Umeng analytics and crash reporting; membership uses Huawei AppGallery payment

Xiaomi

includes Umeng analytics and crash reporting; membership is purchased through the Xiaomi app store (app joint operation)

Why there is no direct download link

  1. ClearDeck does not publish a self-hosted APK, F-Droid build or GitHub Releases.
  2. Each of the four builds is distributed by its own app store, which handles signing and installation.
  3. This page will list the store links as each channel is confirmed.
what we will not do It will not put a server in the middle. ClearDeck has no remote node and nothing that could relay traffic — that is a property of the architecture, not a setting you have to trust.

Where to look

Google Play, Huawei AppGallery, Xiaomi GetApps and OPPO Software Store — rolling out now.
