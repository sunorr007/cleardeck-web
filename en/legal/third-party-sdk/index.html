Third-Party SDK List

The third-party SDKs in each ClearDeck build that process personal data, and the third-party services used by this website.

This list sets out the third-party SDKs in each build of the ClearDeck Android app (the “App”) that process personal data, and the third-party services used by this website. It supplements the Privacy Policy.

Scope

The App is released in four builds for different app stores: Huawei, Xiaomi, Google and OPPO. Each build contains a different set of third-party SDKs, as described in the corresponding section below.

This list covers only third-party SDKs that process personal data. Base development libraries (such as HTTP clients and UI frameworks) are not listed.

Builds

Build Third-party SDKs included
Huawei Umeng (U-App, U-APM), Huawei account and payment components
Xiaomi Umeng (U-App, U-APM), Xiaomi account and payment components (app joint operation)
Google None
OPPO Alipay client SDK (in-app payment)

U-APM (crash and performance) Umeng 2.1.0 Java and native crash information, ANR information To identify and fix stability issues https://www.umeng.com/page/policy

The membership service backend for the Huawei build is operated by us. The data it processes is described in the “Membership Service and Payment” section of the Privacy Policy.

Initialisation and Consent

  • The Umeng SDK performs a pre-initialisation at app startup that does not collect data. Only after you accept the Privacy Policy do we start actual collection.
  • The Huawei account and payment components are used only when you open the membership screen or make a purchase.

Xiaomi Build

SDK Provider Version Data processed Purpose Privacy policy
U-App (analytics) Umeng 9.9.10 Device model, system version, feature usage statistics To understand device compatibility and feature usage https://www.umeng.com/page/policy
U-APM (crash and performance) Umeng 2.1.0 Java and native crash information, ANR information To identify and fix stability issues https://www.umeng.com/page/policy
U-APM support library (asms) Umeng 1.8.7.2 Provides base capability for the components above To support analytics and crash analysis https://www.umeng.com/page/policy

The payment and account components are only invoked when you purchase or restore membership. The data processed by the membership service backend is described in the “Membership Service and Payment” section of the Privacy Policy. Lifetime membership is bound to the device; monthly and yearly memberships can be restored with your Xiaomi account.

Initialisation and Consent

  • The Umeng SDK performs a pre-initialisation at app startup that does not collect data. Only after you accept the Privacy Policy do we start actual collection.
  • The Xiaomi payment and account components are only invoked when you initiate a purchase or restore membership; they are not initialised at app startup.

Google Build

The Google build does not integrate third-party SDKs that process personal data. It contains no analytics, advertising, push or crash reporting components, and no membership purchasing or payment capability.

Initialisation and Consent

  • This build contains no analytics or payment components, so no third-party SDK pre-initialisation or post-consent collection takes place.

OPPO Build

SDK Provider Version Data processed Purpose Privacy policy
Alipay client SDK (in-app payment) Alipay (Alipay.com Co., Ltd.) 15.8.42 Transaction and order information, device information (device model, OS version, etc.), network information To complete membership purchases and payments in the OPPO build https://opendocs.alipay.com/open/01l92q/

Membership in the OPPO build is paid through Alipay. The App does not access your Alipay account or payment details: the order string is signed by our server and handed to the Alipay client to complete the payment.

This build has no channel account system. Membership is bound to this device using an irreversible hash of ANDROID_ID (see the Privacy Policy); that hash is never sent to Alipay. The membership service backend is operated by us. The data it processes is described in the “Membership Service and Payment” section of the Privacy Policy.

Initialisation and Consent

  • The Alipay client SDK is used only when you open the membership screen and start a purchase. This build contains no analytics components, so no post-consent collection takes place.

Processing Before Consent

  • Before you accept the Privacy Policy, the App does not collect data for analytics purposes.
  • Before you accept the Privacy Policy, the App requests the rule list manifest once to check for updates. That request does not contain a Device Identifier or account. See the “Rule List Updates” section of the Privacy Policy.

Membership Data Processing

The Membership Service involves the following Processing:

  • Huawei AppGallery processes payments. We do not access or store your payment method, bank card number or billing address.
  • To verify orders, bind and restore membership, and calculate the trial period, the App sends the Huawei account openId, order receipt, product identifier, SHA-256 hash of ANDROID_ID and trial start to our membership service.
  • This data is used only to provide the Membership Service. Membership data is kept separate from access records.

Membership Data Processing

The Membership Service involves the following Processing:

  • Alipay processes payments. We do not access your Alipay account information, payment method, bank card number or billing address.
  • To verify orders and calculate the trial period, the App sends the order number, product identifier, SHA-256 hash of ANDROID_ID and trial start to our membership service. To credit occasional payments that were not delivered, it also sends the Alipay transaction number you provide.
  • This data is used only to provide the Membership Service. Membership data is kept separate from access records.

Membership Data Processing

The Membership Service involves the following Processing:

  • The Xiaomi app store (app joint operation) processes payments. We do not access your payment method, bank card number or billing address.
  • To verify orders, bind and restore membership, and calculate the trial period, the App sends the Xiaomi account uid, order number, product identifier, SHA-256 hash of ANDROID_ID and trial start to our membership service.
  • Lifetime membership is bound to the purchasing device using the device identifier hash; monthly and yearly memberships can be restored across devices with your Xiaomi account.
  • This data is used only to provide the Membership Service. Membership data is kept separate from access records.

Data Sharing and Retention

Except as described in this list and the Privacy Policy, we do not provide Personal Information to third parties.

Where this build includes third-party SDKs, they retain data for the periods set out in their own privacy policies. To understand a specific retention period, refer to the relevant provider’s privacy policy, or contact us at the email address in the “Contact” section.

Your Choices

  • Without accepting the Privacy Policy, the App cannot run.
  • After you uninstall the App, it no longer collects usage analytics or crash information.
  • To request deletion of membership-related data, contact us at the email address in the “Contact” section.

Changes
