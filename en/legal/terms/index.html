Service Description

The App is a local network filtering tool for Android devices. The App uses Android’s VpnService to establish a local TUN connection, compares each app’s connections against the Rule Lists, and rejects connections that match. Rule matching and blocking decisions are performed on your device.

The App is not What that means
A VPN service The App does not provide a remote node, relay server or subscription service, and your network traffic does not leave your device through us.
An anonymisation service The App does not change your IP address or hide your traffic from your network operator or the operator of the network you connect to.
A security guarantee The App blocks only connections matched by the Rule Lists or rules you import, and cannot guarantee that all harmful connections are blocked.
A monitoring service The App does not send your activity to anyone. Access records are stored only on your device, and you decide whether to export or clear them.

Deep Inspection and the Certificate

Deep Inspection is optional and disabled by default. Enabling it requires you to install the ClearDeck certificate on your device so that the device presents encrypted connections to the App.

  • The certificate is generated on your device and does not leave it.
  • After installing a user certificate, the App is technically capable of viewing the decrypted content of intercepted connections. This is inherent to the technology.
  • Content seen during inspection is used only to make a local block or allow decision and is not uploaded.
  • You may limit the apps to which Deep Inspection applies, restrict it to Wi-Fi, and cap the content inspected per connection.
  • You may remove the certificate from your device to disable Deep Inspection; other features are unaffected.

Whether to enable Deep Inspection is your decision. You bear the risks arising from enabling it, unless applicable law provides otherwise on a mandatory basis.

Consequences of “Block Connections Without VPN”

Android provides a “Block connections without VPN” option. If you enable this option for the App, then whenever the protection engine is not running, your device cannot establish any network connection, and the device will appear to be offline.

This is the intended behaviour of the Android option, not a defect of the App. By enabling the option, you understand and accept this consequence. You may disable the option in system settings at any time.
