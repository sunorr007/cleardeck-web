Privacy Policy

How ClearDeck handles personal information in the Android app and on this website, including purposes, processors and your rights.

This Policy applies to the ClearDeck Android app (the “App”) published and operated by Beijing Fenghong Technology Co., Ltd. (“we”, “us” or “our”).

This Policy also applies to the website at cleardeck.fenghongtech.cn (the “Website”).

Definitions

Term Meaning
Personal Information Information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that is recorded electronically or otherwise, excluding anonymised information.
Processing Any operation performed on Personal Information, including collection, storage, use, adaptation, transmission, provision, disclosure and deletion.
the App The ClearDeck Android app published and operated by us.
Rule Lists Advertising, tracking and malicious-site filtering rules bundled with the App or imported by you.
Deep Inspection An optional feature of the App that matches rules against the full request address and requires you to install a certificate on your device.
Membership Service The paid features and related services offered in the Huawei, OPPO and Xiaomi builds of the App.
Device Identifier The value obtained by applying SHA-256 to the ANDROID_ID provided by the Android system.

Scope and Data Controller

This Policy applies to our Processing of Personal Information through the App. The controller of Personal Information processed through the App is Beijing Fenghong Technology Co., Ltd. Contact details are set out in the “Contact” section below.

This Policy does not apply to products or services independently provided by third parties. The Processing activities of third-party processors are governed by their respective privacy policies.

On-Device Network Processing

The App uses Android’s VpnService to establish a local TUN connection for inspecting and deciding on network connections made by apps on your device. Rule matching and blocking decisions are performed on your device. We do not operate a remote node or relay server for routing your network traffic.

To prevent the App’s own network requests from being captured by itself, the App excludes itself from the TUN capture scope. This Processing does not involve transmitting the content of your network traffic to us.

Permissions We Use

The App only requests the system permissions necessary for its features. Each permission is used for:

Permission Why it is needed
Network access To carry network traffic allowed by protection, and to download rule list updates and verify membership orders.
Network state To detect switches between Wi-Fi and mobile networks and keep protection working after a network change.
Notifications To keep a persistent notification showing the protection status and to alert you if protection is stopped by the system.
View all installed apps To identify which app is making a network connection, so that network access and blocking rules can be shown per app.
Foreground service To keep protection running after you leave the App, so it is not stopped early by the system.
VPN connection (system dialog) To establish the local traffic-filtering channel; it is the prerequisite for blocking ads and trackers.

Channel-specific:

  • Huawei build only: to check and install updates provided by AppGallery inside the app, the system permissions required by the AppGallery update component are used.
  • Huawei and Xiaomi builds only: the analytics component reads device identifiers (such as advertising identifiers) for attribution, and is activated only after you accept this Policy.
  • In some builds, the analytics or payment components request the network state permission for their own network checks.

Information We Do Not Process

We do not collect, upload or store:

  • The content of your network traffic, including the websites you visit, the requests your apps make and the content of your communications.
  • The list of websites or domains you visit.
  • The contents of your access records.
  • Any content of a Deep Inspection connection other than the local decision to block or allow it.
  • Contacts, messages, photos, files, precise location or other Personal Information. The App does not request permission to access such information.

Rule List Updates

When the App starts, returns to the foreground, or you manually request a check, the App requests the rule list manifest from cleardeck-dl.fenghongtech.cn, which we operate, to determine whether a newer version exists.

This request may occur before you accept this Policy, because the App performs a manifest check at startup. The request does not contain the app version, Device Identifier, account or any other identifier. Our server receives and records the IP address, request time, request path and standard HTTP headers.

Automatic downloads of rule module packages occur only on unmetered networks. When you manually request a check from the modules screen, that check is not subject to the unmetered-network restriction. Once downloaded to your device, filtering continues entirely on your device.

Usage Analytics and Crash Analysis

Only the Huawei and Xiaomi builds of the App integrate Umeng’s U-App and U-APM components.

The Umeng SDK performs a pre-initialisation at app startup that does not collect data. Only after you accept this Policy do we start actual collection. The information collected includes device model, system version, feature usage statistics, Java and native crash information and ANR information. It is used to understand device compatibility and feature usage, and to identify and fix stability issues. Umeng processes this information on our behalf. Its privacy policy is available at https://www.umeng.com/page/policy.

The Google and OPPO builds do not integrate the Umeng SDK and do not perform the collection described above.

Membership Service and Payment

The Membership Service is offered in the Huawei, OPPO and Xiaomi builds.

In the Huawei build, payment is processed by Huawei AppGallery. We do not access or store your payment method, bank card number or billing address. To verify orders, bind membership, support restoration of monthly and yearly membership across devices, and calculate the trial period on a per-device basis, the App sends the following information to our membership service: Huawei account openId, order receipt, product identifier, SHA-256 hash of ANDROID_ID and trial start.

In the OPPO build, payment is processed by Alipay. The App does not access your Alipay account or payment details: the order string is signed by our server and handed to the Alipay client to complete the payment. To verify orders, attribute membership to your device and calculate the trial period, the App sends the following information to that membership service: order number, product identifier, SHA-256 hash of ANDROID_ID and trial start; to credit occasional payments that were not delivered, it also sends the Alipay transaction number you provide. The OPPO build has no channel account system, so membership (including lifetime membership) is bound to this device using the device identifier hash.

In the Xiaomi build, payment is processed by the Xiaomi app store (app joint operation). We do not access or store your payment method, bank card number or billing address. To verify orders, bind membership, support restoration of monthly and yearly membership across devices, and calculate the trial period on a per-device basis, the App sends the following information to our membership service: Xiaomi account uid, order number, product identifier, SHA-256 hash of ANDROID_ID and trial start. Lifetime membership is bound to the purchasing device using the device identifier hash.

This information is used only to provide the Membership Service, prevent reuse of the same order, bind lifetime membership to the purchasing device and calculate the trial period. Membership data is kept separate from access records, and the membership service cannot learn which websites you visit or the contents of your access records.

Deep Inspection

Deep Inspection is optional and disabled by default. When enabled, the certificate is generated on your device and does not leave it. Content seen during inspection is used only to make a local block or allow decision and is not uploaded.

You may limit Deep Inspection to selected apps, restrict it to Wi-Fi, and cap the content inspected per connection (256 KB by default). Removing the certificate from your device disables the feature.

On-Device Data

The following data is stored only on your device and is not uploaded:

Data Storage location Deletion
Access records (app, host and decision) Local database Cleared when protection stops; can be exported as a CSV file before stopping
Settings and block/allow rules Local storage Deleted when the App is uninstalled
Rule lists and traffic statistics Local database Deleted when the App is uninstalled
Membership status snapshot Local database Deleted when the App is uninstalled

You can export access records as a CSV file from the “Tools” screen. The file is saved in your device storage and is not sent to us.

Sharing and Cross-Border Transfer

We do not sell your Personal Information. Except as described in this Policy, we do not provide your Personal Information to third parties, unless otherwise required by laws or regulations or separately agreed by you.

If any Processing involves a cross-border transfer of Personal Information, we will complete the procedures required by applicable law and adopt necessary safeguards.

Information Security

We adopt technical and organisational measures appropriate to the App’s functions to protect Personal Information, including HTTPS transmission, data minimisation, keeping network filtering and access records on your device, and restricting internal access.

No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure. If a Personal Information security incident occurs, we will take measures and fulfil notification obligations as required by applicable law.

Your Rights

To the extent permitted by applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access and obtain a copy of your Personal Information;
  • request correction of inaccurate Personal Information;
  • request deletion of your Personal Information;
  • withdraw your consent;
  • request an explanation of our Personal Information Processing rules;
  • lodge a complaint about our Processing.

You may exercise these rights by contacting us at the email address in the “Contact” section. We will respond within the period required by law.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect the validity of Processing carried out on the basis of your consent before withdrawal. After you uninstall the App, it will no longer collect usage analytics or crash information.

Protection of Minors

The App is not directed to children under the age of 14. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under 14. If we discover that we have collected a child’s Personal Information without the consent of a guardian, we will delete it in accordance with law.

The Website

The Website is static and does not provide a login function.

Launch Notification List

The launch notification form on the Website has been retired, and the Website no longer collects new email addresses. Addresses submitted through that form before it was retired are used only to send a release notification. They are not used for marketing, and are not sold or provided to unrelated third parties. To delete an address you submitted earlier, contact us at the email address in the “Contact” section.

Analytics

The Website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure visits. This service does not use cookies and reports only aggregate page views and referrers. It does not fingerprint individual visitors or track you across websites.

The Website does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, social-media trackers or other third-party scripts.

Cookies and Local Storage

The Website does not set its own cookies. Your language choice and page-effect preference are stored in your browser’s localStorage, remain on your device and are not transmitted to us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy in response to changes in laws, regulations, business or technology. The updated Policy will be published on the Website, and the version number, last updated date and effective date at the top of the document will be updated. If a change materially affects your rights, we will notify you through a notice on the Website or another reasonable method.

Contact

If you have any question, comment or complaint about this Policy or our Processing of Personal Information, contact us at: support@fenghongtech.cn.

This Policy is governed by the laws of the People’s Republic of China. Any dispute arising from this Policy shall be resolved through negotiation; if negotiation fails, it shall be submitted to the competent people’s court in Beijing.
