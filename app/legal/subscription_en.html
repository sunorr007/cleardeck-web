Membership Content

The App’s basic protection features are provided free of charge, including the Rule Lists, per-app allow rules, access records and the entry point to Deep Inspection.

The Membership Service unlocks the following three Restricted Modules:

  • Ads blocking
  • Deep ads blocking
  • Malicious site blocking

The Restricted Modules are disabled by default, and not having a membership does not affect the use of other features of the App.

Free Trial

  • The Trial Period is 14 days, beginning when you first enable any Restricted Module.
  • During the Trial Period you may use all three Restricted Modules in full.
  • A trial is available once per device. Uninstalling and reinstalling the App does not restart the Trial Period.
  • When the Trial Period ends, the Restricted Modules are suspended and other features of the App are unaffected.

Purchase and Term

Plan Term of one purchase Renewal
Monthly membership 30 days Manual renewal
Yearly membership 365 days Manual renewal
Lifetime membership Valid indefinitely, bound to the purchasing device No renewal required
  • Monthly and yearly memberships do not renew automatically and do not charge automatically. You need to purchase again after the term ends.
  • If you purchase the same plan again while it is still valid, the additional term is added consecutively from the end of the existing term.

Binding Method

Huawei build:

  • Monthly and yearly memberships are bound to your Huawei account. After changing devices, sign in with the same Huawei account and use “Restore my membership” to restore your benefits.
  • Lifetime membership is bound to the device used at purchase and does not transfer to other devices with the Huawei account; after changing devices you need to purchase again on the new device.

OPPO build:

  • Membership is bound to your device: this build has no channel account system, so monthly, yearly and lifetime memberships are all bound to the device used at purchase.
  • If you uninstall and reinstall the App on the same device, your membership is restored automatically; you do not need to purchase again.
  • Membership cannot be restored automatically on a new device. To move a membership to a new device, contact us at the email address in the “Contact” section with your Alipay transaction number; we will handle it manually after verification.
